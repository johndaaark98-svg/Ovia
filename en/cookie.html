Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which types are used by the website oviaitalia.it and how you can manage your preferences. The data controller is L3 Innovation S.r.l. (VAT no. IT02882330901), who can be contacted at oviaitalia@gmail.com.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites send to the user’s device, where they are stored and sent back to the same sites on the next visit. They can be set by the site owner (“first-party cookies”) or by third parties (“third-party cookies”).

2. Types of cookies used

Technical (necessary) cookies

These are essential for the Site to work properly and to provide the services requested by the user. They do not require consent. They may include cookies that store your cookie consent preferences.

Statistical and third-party cookies

The Site currently does not use profiling cookies for advertising purposes. If statistical measurement tools (for example analytics services) or third-party components that set cookies are introduced in the future, they will only be activated with your prior consent, collected through the banner, and this page will be updated accordingly.

3. Managing your preferences

On your first visit to the Site, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choices at any time by deleting cookies from your browser. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies through their settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that disabling technical cookies may affect the proper functioning of the Site.

4. More information

For information on the processing of other personal data, see the Privacy Policy. For any questions you can write to oviaitalia@gmail.com.
