Frequently asked questions

The questions people ask us before getting started.

What is Ovia, in plain terms?

Ovia builds tailor-made artificial intelligence systems for professional firms and SMEs in Italy. They handle repetitive work, such as email, documents, calls, deadlines and new enquiries, while you and your team focus on clients. We don’t sell software to install: first we observe how you work, then we build the system around you.

How are you different from companies selling software or a chatbot?

In where we start. Anyone today can buy a subscription to an AI tool. The value lies in deciding what to automate, how to protect your clients’ data and how to measure the result. For us, strategy and security make the money; AI is the tool. That’s why every project starts from an analysis of your workflow, not from a product demo.

What is the Process Check?

It’s the first step: thirty minutes on a video call about your real workflow. We look at where the time goes, what gets repeated and where information gets lost. At the end you know which tasks can be safely automated, how much time can be recovered and where it’s best to start. No jargon.

Is my clients’ data used to train ChatGPT or other models?

No. We use providers and configurations that exclude the use of data for model training, and we put it in writing. Your clients’ information stays in your system and isn’t read by anyone outside your firm.

Can I use AI in my firm? What does Italian Law 132/2025 say?

Yes. Art. 13 of Italian Law 132/2025, in force since 10 October 2025, allows professionals to use AI for instrumental and support activities, provided that intellectual work remains predominant and the client is informed in clear language. Ovia systems are designed exactly this way: the system proposes, the professional decides. We cover it in detail in this article.

What services do you offer?

Seven systems that can be combined: Ovia Second Brain (the firm’s memory), Ovia Inbox (email), Ovia Lead Generation (new clients), Ovia Calls (from phone call to action), Ovia Documents (collection and reminders), Ovia AI-Ready Websites and Ovia CRM.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we build, because every system is tailor-made. In general there’s a set-up fee and a monthly fee covering maintenance, updates and human support. We give you precise figures only after the Process Check, when we know what’s really needed and how much time it saves you.

How long before I see the first results?

The first working core usually arrives 3–4 weeks after the Process Check, on one area and a small group of clients. We start small on purpose: first we measure, then we extend.
