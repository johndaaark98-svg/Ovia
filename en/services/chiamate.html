The problem

The most important decisions are made on the phone. And that’s where they stay.

A twenty-minute call with a client often creates another fifteen minutes of work: notes to tidy up, tasks to hand to colleagues, dates to record, a summary email to write. When the day is full, that work slips, or doesn’t get done. And what was said becomes “I think we said…”.

Transcription technology is now mature and widespread: in Italy, speech recognition is among the most common uses of AI in business [1]. But recording a call with a client isn’t a neutral act: it needs a privacy notice, a legal basis and proper retention. That’s why almost half of the companies that give up on AI cite privacy [1].

Ovia starts here: first clear rules on what gets recorded, how it’s communicated and where the data lives. Then the automation that gives you back those fifteen minutes for every call.

Strategy and security make the money. AI is the tool. The dot marks where you decide.

Anyone today can buy a subscription to an AI tool. The value lies in deciding what to automate, how to protect your clients’ data and how to measure the result. That’s where we always start.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

Can I record calls with clients?

Yes, under specific conditions: inform the other party, have a legitimate purpose, retain the data properly and limit access. In the project we set up notices and procedures before enabling any recording.

Do I need special equipment?

No. It works with phone systems, video calls and dedicated recorders (such as Plaud), depending on how you work. We pick the simplest option for your firm.

Does transcription work well, including technical terms?

Yes, current systems handle Italian and English well; for specialist terminology and client names we add a dedicated vocabulary. The summary can always be reviewed and corrected.

Where do the recordings end up?

In the firm’s system, with per-client access, time-limited retention and no use for training external models.

Does the client receive the summary automatically?

Only after your approval. The system prepares it, you decide whether and when to send it.
