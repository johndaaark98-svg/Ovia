The problem

Your firm’s knowledge exists. The problem is that it’s scattered.

A decision made on the phone, a commitment made in a meeting, a client detail on a sticky note, an exception agreed by email two years ago. Every day your firm produces valuable knowledge, and every day some of it is lost: in the head of whoever was there, in inboxes nobody else reads, in folders nobody opens.

The cost doesn’t appear on the balance sheet, but it’s real: hours spent reconstructing, questions that interrupt the partner, avoidable mistakes, clients who have to repeat themselves. According to McKinsey, almost a fifth of the working week goes just on searching for internal information [1].

The point isn’t buying yet another AI tool. It’s deciding what needs to be remembered, who can consult it and where the data lives. Strategy and security first. Then the tool.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

How is it different from an archive or a document management system?

An archive stores files; a second brain stores meaning. It links information from different sources to the same client, extracts commitments and decisions, and answers plain-language questions citing the source. Your existing software stays: the second brain works alongside it.

Can the AI make up answers?

The risk exists with any model, which is why the system only answers using your firm’s content and always shows the source. If the information isn’t there, it says so. Human review remains the final step.

Will my clients’ data end up training ChatGPT or similar?

No. We use providers and configurations that exclude the use of data for training, and where needed we offer a dedicated installation. It’s the first thing we put in writing.

Do I have to tell my clients that I use AI?

For intellectual professions in Italy, art. 13 of Law 132/2025 requires informing clients about the AI systems used, in clear language, and keeping human intellectual work predominant. We help you set up the disclosure and processes consistently.

How long before I see a result?

The first working core usually arrives in 3–4 weeks, on a small group of clients. We start small on purpose: first we measure, then we extend.

Does it work with the tools I already use?

Yes, that’s the principle of the Ovia method: we connect the email, phone system, folders and practice software you already use, instead of asking you to change them.
