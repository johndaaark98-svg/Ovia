Ovia Inbox

Ovia Inbox reads every incoming email, works out which client it’s from, what it’s about and how urgent it is, assigns it to the right person and prepares a draft reply in your firm’s tone. No important email buried, no reply sent without your yes.

The problem

Your inbox has become your desk. And nobody keeps it tidy.

Client requests, notices from public bodies, certified email, suppliers, newsletters, reminders: everything lands in the same place, at the same speed, with the same apparent priority. The result is that whoever opens the inbox spends the morning sorting instead of working.

The temptation is to paste emails into a chatbot to get replies written. That is exactly the “shadow AI” which, according to IBM, drives up the cost of data breaches: only 31% of Italian organisations have effective rules to manage it [2].

Ovia Inbox does the opposite: first we decide your firm’s rules, priorities, tone and permissions, then we put AI to work inside that perimeter, with human approval as the mandatory final step.

To the right person, immediately

Step 4 of 5 · Prepares

A draft reply in your firm’s tone

Step 5 of 5 · Approve

You review, edit and send

No reply goes out without human approval. Every message sent stays in the client’s history.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

Are emails sent automatically?

No, by default nothing goes out without approval. You can choose to automate only low-risk messages, such as receipt confirmations.

Does it work with certified email (PEC), Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. We connect to the inboxes you already use, including PEC, without asking you to migrate.

How does it write in my firm’s tone?

We start from your real replies and templates you approve. The tone is set as a rule, and every draft can be edited before sending.

Who can read my clients’ emails?

Only authorised people in your firm. The system is configured with per-person permissions and the data isn’t used to train external models.

What if the AI misclassifies an email?

You correct it with one click and the rule improves. In the first weeks we monitor the classifications together for exactly this reason.
