The problem

You don’t lose clients because you’re too expensive. You lose them because you reply late.

A prospective client fills in the form, writes on WhatsApp or calls. At that moment they are deciding. If the reply arrives the next day, they’ve already written to two other firms. Harvard Business Review research on more than two thousand companies is blunt: average response time 42 hours, and almost one company in four doesn’t reply at all [1].

Then there are quotes sent and never followed up, and contacts that have “gone cold”. They aren’t lost: nobody has contacted them again in the right way.

Lead generation isn’t a piece of software, it’s a strategy: who your ideal client is, what you say to them, when to follow up and when to stop. Ovia designs it with you, writes it down, then has the system carry it out every day, without exceptions.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

Is it legal to contact leads who haven’t replied for months?

It depends on the legal basis on which you collected the contact and on what your privacy notice said. In the Process Check we review the database and only reactivate contacts where it’s correct to do so. Anyone who refused consent stays excluded.

Will my prospects realise an AI is replying?

Yes, and they should: transparency is an Ovia rule and, from 2 August 2026, also an EU AI Act obligation for people interacting with an AI system. The goal isn’t to pretend to be human, but to reply immediately and well, then hand over to you.

Do you buy or generate contact lists?

No. We work on the enquiries that come to you and the database you already have. Bought lists are a legal and reputational risk we don’t let our clients take.

How quickly will I see results?

Instant replies show effects in the first weeks, because they reduce lost enquiries. Follow-up and reactivation need a full sales cycle to be measured.

Does it integrate with my CRM?

Yes. If you already use a CRM we connect to it; if you don’t have one, Ovia CRM is built precisely to work alongside lead generation.
