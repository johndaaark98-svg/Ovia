The problem

Ranking first on Google isn’t enough any more. You need to be cited.

When an AI-generated summary appears on Google, the top result loses on average 58% of its clicks: Ahrefs measured it across 300,000 keywords [1]. Meanwhile, more and more people ask ChatGPT or Gemini directly “which accountant in Treviso works with start-ups?”. AI gives one answer, not ten links.

Academic research says this visibility can be earned: content optimised for generative engines (GEO) gains up to 40% more visibility in answers [2]. But not with tricks: with clear, authoritative, structured and verifiable content.

Beware the shortcut: filling your site with mass-produced AI pages is exactly what Google classifies as “scaled content abuse” and penalises [3]. You need an editorial strategy, not a text factory. That’s what we build.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?

SEO works to rank you among Google’s results; GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) works to get you chosen and cited as a source in AI-generated answers. The foundations are shared (useful content, a technically sound site), but GEO especially rewards clear answers, data, citations and structure.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will cite me?

No, and be wary of anyone who does: nobody controls AI engines. We can measurably increase the likelihood of being cited, with methods documented by research, and measure the results.

Do you write the content with AI?

AI helps with production, but every piece of content starts from a strategy, contains your real expertise and is reviewed by a person. Publishing mass-produced text with no value is what Google penalises.

I already have a website. Do I have to rebuild it from scratch?

Not always. After the Process Check we tell you frankly whether it’s better to improve your existing site or rebuild it.

How long before I see results?

The new site goes live in a few weeks; rankings and citations grow over months, with consistent content. That’s why we work with a monthly care and growth fee.
