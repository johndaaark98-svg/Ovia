The problem

A CRM nobody updates is just another spreadsheet.

Almost every company has tried a CRM. It almost always ends the same way: for the first few months everyone fills it in, then it becomes one more chore, then it’s back to the spreadsheet, the paper list on the desk, the partner’s memory. Salespeople spend less than a third of their time selling and the rest on admin and data entry [1].

The problem isn’t the software: it’s that a standard CRM asks you to adapt to it, and to feed it by hand.

Ovia CRM flips the logic: first we design your process (clients, cases, deadlines, roles), then we build the CRM around it and connect it to email, calls and documents so it updates itself. And the data stays where you decide, on-premise too.

Strategy and security make the money. AI is the tool. The dot marks where you decide.

Anyone today can buy a subscription to an AI tool. The value lies in deciding what to automate, how to protect your clients’ data and how to measure the result. That’s where we always start.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

Why not use an off-the-shelf CRM?

You can, and for many that works fine. Ovia CRM makes sense when your process is specific (like a professional firm’s), when you want it to update itself from email and calls, or when you want the data on-premise.

Can I import the data I already have?

Yes: we import spreadsheets, exports from your practice software and address books, and clean them up before launch.

Is training needed?

Very little, because the CRM mirrors how you work. We still run launch sessions with the team, and the fee includes hours of human support every month.

Is my clients’ data safe?

Role-based permissions, a change log, backups and the option of an on-premise installation. The data is never used to train external models.

Does it integrate with the other Ovia services?

That’s what it’s designed for: Inbox, Calls, Documents and Lead Generation feed the CRM, and Second Brain makes it all searchable.
