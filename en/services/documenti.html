The problem

Your firm’s work grinds to a halt over a document that never arrives.

The tax return is ready, only one receipt is missing. The contract needs registering, but the second signatory’s ID hasn’t arrived. Every stalled case generates calls, emails, WhatsApp messages, reminders on paper. Multiplied across hundreds of clients, it’s a full-time job nobody chose to do.

Then there are the documents that are always filled in the same way: powers of attorney, lease agreements, engagement letters. Skilled time spent copying data from one file to another.

The solution isn’t “an AI that reads PDFs”: it’s a document strategy for each type of case (what’s needed, by when, how to remind) made secure and then automated. Extracting text and documents is already the most common use of AI in Italian companies [1]; what makes the difference is how you govern it.

Strategy and security make the money. AI is the tool. The dot marks where you decide.

Anyone today can buy a subscription to an AI tool. The value lies in deciding what to automate, how to protect your clients’ data and how to measure the result. That’s where we always start.

FAQ

The questions people really ask us.

My clients aren’t tech-savvy. Will it still work?

Yes. The client receives a simple message with the list of documents and a link to upload them, even from a smartphone. Anyone who prefers can keep bringing them to the office: the system still records what has arrived.

Does the system really recognise whether a document is the right one?

It recognises the document type and checks key elements (holder, period, legibility). Doubtful cases are flagged to a person instead of being accepted automatically.

Can I choose the tone of the reminders?

Yes, it’s part of the strategy: we define the texts and escalation together, from a polite reminder to a formal notice.

Does it integrate with my practice software?

We connect your existing software where possible; otherwise Ovia Documents works with Ovia CRM, which acts as the case dashboard.

Can I generate my own contract templates?

Yes: we start from your real templates. The system fills them with the collected data and gives you back a draft to review and sign.
